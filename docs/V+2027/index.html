Why now

Ready for the AI-native supply chain

OEMs are building digital, AI-enabled factories and they expect the same discipline from their suppliers. The shops that can prove quality, protect data and deliver on time will win the next decade of work.

North Carolina

A new aerospace supply base is forming

JetZero broke ground in Greensboro in 2026 on a Z4 plant planned around Siemens, Nvidia and AWS digital platforms. Regional suppliers will be measured against that bar.

Defense

CMMC is paused, the risk is not

Phase 2 third-party assessments are on hold, but Phase 1 self-assessments, DFARS 7012, SPRS affirmations and False Claims Act exposure all still apply. Primes set their own deadlines.

Operations

Capacity is the quiet deal-breaker

Buyers qualify suppliers who can scale on schedule. A hidden constraint on one machine or one approval step can cap a whole plant's output.
